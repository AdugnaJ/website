Identity

Bolale Design Lab is a contemporary fashion brand focused on creating ready-to-wear clothing that blends modern streetwear with Ethiopian cultural identity. The brand explores innovative ways of working with traditional Ethiopian fabrics, reimagining them through contemporary design . It also creates limited-edition eco-dyed pieces, combining sustainability, craftsmanship, and artistic experimentation to produce timeless garments with purpose and character.
